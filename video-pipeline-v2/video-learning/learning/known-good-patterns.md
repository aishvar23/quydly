# Known good patterns

Each entry: `[<date> story <id>] <one-line pattern> — replicate: <how to>`.
Read by every fresh Claude before stage 3 and stage 4. Append-only.

When a pattern repeats across types, promote a template edit in
`video-learning/templates/<type>.json` or
`video-learning/templates/module-skeletons/<kind>.md`.

## Seed entries

- [seed] Hooks that lead with a proper noun + concrete verb in the first
  4 words land harder than time/place-first hooks. — replicate: in stage 3,
  draft 2 candidate hooks and prefer the one whose first 4 words contain
  a proper noun.
- [seed] Stakes lines that name the affected population *with a number*
  outperform abstract stakes. — replicate: in stage 3, the Stakes
  paragraph must contain at least one digit-bearing token.
- [seed] Three- or four-module evidence runs (vs. five+) rate higher in
  post-render review on 60–75s scripts. — replicate: in stage 4, prefer
  3 evidence modules unless the story has a quote *and* a map module, in
  which case 4 is fine.
