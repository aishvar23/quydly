# Module: stakes

The "why now / who is affected" beat. Lives between hook and evidence.

## Renderer contract
- `kind: "stakes"`
- `duration_sec`: 4.0–8.0
- `text`: 1–2 sentences, ≤ 40 words
- `asset_hint`: `map` | `data` | `mute`
- `evidence_ref`: at least one source_id (usually a number-bearing fact)

## Beat structure
1. **Affected population** — named with a noun and a number where possible
   ("3.2 million households", "the 18 million expats in the Gulf").
2. **Time window or trigger** — what makes this matter *now*
   ("ahead of the Friday deadline", "with the rainy season starting in
   three weeks").

## Banned constructions
- "the world", "everyone", "many people"
- "investors will be watching", "tensions rise"
- Future speculation as fact

## Common pitfalls
- Stakes that just restate the hook with extra words.
- Stakes that moralise ("a deeply troubling situation").
- Stakes that drift away from the cited evidence — every claim must trace.
